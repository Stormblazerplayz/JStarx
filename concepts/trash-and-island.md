# Trash can and the island drop zones

Status: written down, not built yet.

## Trash can in the dock
- A trash can icon sits in the dock.
- Deleted folders and apps go to the trash first. Nothing is removed right away.
- Open the trash to restore an item or empty it for good.
- Deleting from the trash (or emptying it) plays the black hole animation: the item is pulled
  into a swirling black hole and shrinks away.

## Dragging files onto the island
- When you drag a file or folder over the Dynamic Island, the island opens into two zones:
  - **Delete**: drop here to send the item to the trash (with the black hole animation).
  - **Store**: drop here to keep the item inside the island, for quick access later.
- Stored items show up in the island, and you can drag them back out onto the desktop.
- If you drag away without dropping, the island closes again.
